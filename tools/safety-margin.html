The margin is a percentage of the supply, not the demand: supply = demand ÷ (1 − margin). Adding 10% of the demand comes up short — it only yields a 9.1% cushion of the resulting supply. Flow doesn't enter the math; the margin applies to pressure at the demand flow point.
